Time

Time is a currency whose unit is the second. The network creates one second of currency for each second that passes on its chain, one for the whole network and not one per person, so the supply always equals the time since the network started.

This is a test network

  • The seconds here have no value. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for anything.
  • The network may be reset at any time, and everything on it erased.
  • The software has not had its outside audit yet.
  • The end date of this test network is not set on this page.

    Who holds time

    #AccountHoldsShare

    Names are shown only for the network's own accounts: the validators' operators, the registration sponsors, the faucet, the demo blog and the staking pools. Every person's account is shown as Anonymous. "Holds" counts what is on the account and what it has staked.

    How these figures are made: the seconds in existence, the accounts and the validators are the chain's own counters, which the network's service reads with a proof. Who holds time is read account by account with proofs. Actions, payments and seconds moved are counted by the service from the blocks it checked: its word, not a proof. The explorer checks blocks in your own browser.

    Your keys in one minute

    Every day
    Your @name and your password. The password unlocks the key that this browser keeps for you.
    Keep safe
    The backup file. With it and your password you can open your account in another browser. Your password alone cannot bring the key back once this browser's data is cleared.
    After "Protect my account"
    Your five recovery words, written by hand on paper: paper is the safest place for them. The wallet shows them only once. Under Keys you can add a Recovery Kit, so that the paper alone is not enough. Anyone who has the paper can ask to take over your account: that waits, and the Alerts at the top of the wallet's Home tell you. Only your recovery words can stop it, so keep two copies in different places.
