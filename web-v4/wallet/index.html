Write down your recovery words Keep safe

These five words bring your account back if you lose your passwords or this device. Take a pen and write them on paper.

Paper is the safest place for them: nothing online can reach it. That is why the wallet saves them nowhere and offers no file here.

On paper only: not in a photo, a note or a message. Nobody can show them to you again.

Write them twice and keep the two papers in different places. If one is lost, the other brings your account back. If one is taken, the other lets you stop whoever took it. The fridge door does not count.

Anyone who has these words can ask to take over your account. That waits your waiting period. You can stop it only with these words, so a paper that is your only copy must never be lost or taken. The Alerts at the top of Home tell you if it happens.

        On this network all new seconds, up to 86,400 a day, go to the validators who sign blocks, by voting power, and through them to the people who stake with them. Stake with a validator below and your part of its reward is added to your stake as it arrives; the validator keeps a commission of at most 20% of it.
